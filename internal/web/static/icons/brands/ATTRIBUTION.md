# Brand icon attribution

The SVG marks in this directory are trademarks of their respective owners and
are used only to identify the corresponding subscription services.

Most marks are sourced from the Simple Icons project (CC0 1.0) and SVGL (MIT).
Simplified marks are bundled for AWS, Prime Video, Google Workspace, Basic-Fit,
Endesa, and Iberdrola, together with a generic briefcase fallback.

All icons are embedded into the Fiscus binary. The dashboard never fetches brand
assets from external hosts at runtime.
