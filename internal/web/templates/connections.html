Connecting a bank

Consent is created once from the command line

  1. Find your institution 
    fiscus institutions -country ES
  2. Create the consent 
    fiscus connect -institution "Banco Santander"
    GoCardless uses the institution ID; Enable Banking uses the bank name.
  3. Finish the redirect Open the printed link, select accounts, and complete your bank's authentication. The consent is stored automatically when the bank redirects back to Fiscus.

Fiscus never initiates payments and only requests read access to balances and transactions.
