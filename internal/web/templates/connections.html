Connecting a bank

Consent is created once from the command line

  1. Find your institution 
    fiscus institutions -country {{"ES"}}
  2. Create the consent 
    fiscus connect -institution <institution-id>
  3. Finish the redirect Open the printed link, select accounts, and complete your bank's authentication. Fiscus is notified on the next scheduled sync.

Fiscus never initiates payments. Access tokens stay in memory and are refreshed automatically.
